HireProof

Screening that verifies the work, never the person.
Why I built it

In a real hiring drive I worked on, only 2 of 35 applicants had genuine hands-on experience in the required domain, and keyword filters couldn't tell them apart. Checking them properly meant reading every resume closely, and pasting them into an AI chat would have exposed applicants' personal data.

The problem

Resumes are written to beat keyword filters, so the right words no longer prove the right experience. And the fastest way to check them, pasting resumes into an AI chat, hands every candidate's name, phone number and personal details to an outside service.

How HireProof solves it
  • Redacts before it reads. Names, phone numbers, IDs and addresses are stripped on the recruiter's own device, and nothing is shared until they approve it.
  • Reads like a domain expert, not a keyword filter. Separates skills shown in real project work from skills only listed, and flags claims a practitioner would find technically wrong or implausible.
  • Does the maths. Claimed years vs actual job dates, gaps and future dates.
  • Spots manipulation. Text copied between applicants, borrowed "About us" text and stuffed skills lists, plus hidden instructions aimed at AI screening tools.
  • Turns doubts into questions. Every odd point comes with the exact question to ask, plus three drill-down questions per candidate and what a real answer sounds like.
  • Keeps people in charge. HireProof recommends; the recruiter reviews the evidence and makes every decision. A red flag or a thin resume means "verify", never "reject".
  • Costs nothing to run. Offline rules, your own AI key, or copy-to-chat, on desktop or phone.
Built with AI pair-programming · Single-file web app · In-browser PDF & Word reading · Rules engine + bring-your-own-key AI · Desktop & mobile
